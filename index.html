PIN HEAD

EMS Synthi AKS in your pocket

A pin-matrix firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

The Synthi

The EMS Synthi AKS (1972) was a portable modular synthesizer in a briefcase. Its 16×16 pin matrix let you patch anything to anything with three colored pins: white (normal), red (precision), green (attenuated). Used by Pink Floyd on Dark Side of the Moon (“On the Run”), Brian Eno, and countless others.

Pin Head brings that exact patching philosophy to the M-VAVE FM-1.

Matrix Demo

Click a cell to cycle pin (none → white → red → green). Knobs 1/2 would scroll on the real device.

Install & Recovery

Install

  1. Download the latest .bin from the releases.
  2. Use M-Flasher or the browser flasher (coming soon).
  3. Hold the correct buttons while powering on if required by the base firmware.

Unbrick / Revert to Stock

  • fm1-custom-fw recovery tools
  • FM-1 Emulator for testing first
  • Official M-VAVE stock firmware via their site or M-Flasher

Always test in the emulator before flashing hardware.
